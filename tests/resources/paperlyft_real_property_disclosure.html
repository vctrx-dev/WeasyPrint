10. Real Property Ownership

What to disclose: Arizona real property, including land and improvements, owned by you or a member of your household during the reporting period, other than your primary residence or property used for personal recreation. Describe the location and approximate size, then identify the approximate value.

If the land was first acquired or completely divested during this period, provide the date and identify whether it was acquired or divested. Otherwise, select not applicable.

You need not disclose: Your primary residence or property used for personal recreation.
